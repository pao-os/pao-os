# PAO OS — Business Operating Systems for SMEs

PAO OS is an AI-native Business Operating System being developed for SMEs in Vietnam, by IVN Network.

It is being built as one operating layer for goals, plans, tasks and reports, with internal knowledge for context-aware AI suggestions and specialist AI agents coordinated under human review and approval.

**Status: Pre-launch — in development.**

This profile does not claim a launched product, customers, revenue, pricing, or a launch date. Features described here are directions being built and validated, not shipped functionality.

## What PAO OS is being built for

- Turn a business goal into a plan, tasks with owners, and tracked outcomes
- Keep planning, execution and reporting in one business context, instead of scattered tools
- Give leaders clearer visibility into progress, ownership and next steps, with AI assisting — not replacing — human decisions

## Who it is for

SME founders, CEOs and marketing teams running repeated marketing, customer engagement and operating workflows without a unified platform.
